2. Billing and auto-renewal

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account (or your Google Play account) when you confirm the purchase. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the same price and for the same period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Free trial (if offered): [N] days, after which the subscription converts to paid automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. If you buy a subscription during a free trial, any unused part of the trial is forfeited. We will remind you before a free trial converts to a paid subscription.
  • Price increases: we will notify you at least 30 days in advance. Where the store or the law requires it (including Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce), the increase applies only if you agree to it. Otherwise your subscription will not renew at the new price.

3. How to cancel

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app or your Mindscape account does not cancel a subscription by itself.

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Mindscape → Cancel Subscription. In Mindscape, Profile → Manage subscription opens the same screen.
  • Android: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Mindscape → Cancel.

4. Refunds

Because purchases are processed by Apple or Google, refunds are handled by the store you paid through, under its policies. We cannot issue refunds for those purchases directly.

  • Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, or contact Apple Support.
  • Google Play: request a refund through Google Play's order history (play.google.com → Order history), or see Google Play's refund policy. Within 48 hours of purchase, Google Play may refund automatically.

If the store asks us to confirm details, or if you can't get a refund you believe you are owed, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will help.

5. Your statutory rights

Korea (청약철회, right of withdrawal)

Under Article 17 of the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, you may withdraw from a purchase within 7 days of buying it. For digital content, this right may be limited once you have started using the paid features, provided we have clearly told you so before purchase and given you a way to try the service first (such as the free tier or a free trial). Withdrawal is always available if the service is materially different from what was advertised, or if it fails to work because of our fault. In that case you may withdraw within 3 months of purchase, or within 30 days of when you discovered (or could have discovered) the problem. Withdrawals for store purchases are processed through Apple or Google as described in section 4.

EU / UK

You normally have a 14-day right of withdrawal for digital services. When you subscribe, you will be asked to agree that the service starts immediately and to acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal once it does, to the extent the law allows.

Everywhere

Nothing in this policy limits refund or withdrawal rights that you have under mandatory consumer protection law.

6. Partial periods and service changes

Unless the law or the store requires otherwise, we do not provide pro-rated refunds for unused time after you cancel. If we permanently discontinue Pro, or remove a core Pro feature, during a period you paid for, we will help you get a pro-rated refund for the unused time through the store you paid through.

7. Restoring purchases

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, open Profile → Manage subscription → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.
