Privacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Last updated November 5, 2026
Draft, not legal advice. This draft describes how Mindscape actually works today and follows Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA. Highlighted fields must be completed, and a qualified lawyer should review the policy before you publish it.
The short version
  • What you write or say in Mindscape is sensitive. It can reveal your thoughts, beliefs and mental state, and we treat it that way.
  • As a guest, your data stays in your browser's storage on your device. If you create an account, it is also synced to a private database that only your login can read.
  • To produce your analysis, the text of an entry is sent to Anthropic, which runs the Claude AI model. Anthropic is our service provider and does not use that text to train its models.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, use third-party analytics or track you across apps or websites.
  • You can download or delete your data at any time from Profile → Data & Privacy.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Entries. The text you type, or speak and have transcribed, in "Speak your mind", plus any aspiration or goal statement you set.
  • Reactions and edits. Whether you accept, reject or refine the beliefs and hypotheses the app suggests.
  • Account information (only if you sign up): your name, your email address and a password. Your password is stored as a one-way hash by our authentication provider. We never see it in plain text.
  • Settings, such as reminder times and notification preferences.
  • Communications you send us, such as support or privacy requests.

2.2 Information the Service generates

  • AI-derived insights: the beliefs, assumptions, connections, hypotheses, summaries and goal "drift" notes generated from your entries.
  • On-device language observations: simple counts computed on your device, such as how often you use first-person pronouns or certain thinking verbs.
  • App state, such as entry counts, whether you've seen the tutorial, and (if paid plans are enabled) your plan status.

2.3 Microphone and voice

If you choose voice input, the app asks for microphone permission. Audio is used on your device to draw the live waveform. Speech-to-text is handled by your browser's or operating system's built-in speech recognition (for example Apple's or Google's), which may send audio to that vendor's servers to return text. That processing is governed by the vendor's own privacy policy. Mindscape never receives, stores or keeps your audio. Only the resulting text becomes part of your entry.

2.4 Technical information

When the app calls our servers, our hosting provider processes standard request data, such as your IP address, request time and browser type, in order to deliver the request and protect the Service from abuse. We do not use this data to build profiles, and it is kept only in short-lived server logs (see section 8).

2.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or your browsing activity outside Mindscape. We use no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

3. Sensitive information

Your entries and the insights generated from them can reveal information about your mental health, beliefs and inner life. Under GDPR Article 9 this is "special category" data, and under PIPA Article 23 it is "sensitive information" (민감정보). We process it only to provide the self-reflection features you request and only with your explicit consent, which we ask for separately before your first analysis. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your data or your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened.

  • Items transferred: the data listed for each recipient in section 6.
  • Destination country: the United States (Anthropic, Vercel). Account and sync data (Supabase) is stored in the Republic of Korea (Seoul).
  • Timing and method: over encrypted network connections (TLS), whenever you request an analysis or your data syncs.

10. Crisis-language detection

Each entry is checked on your device against a fixed list of phrases that may indicate risk of self-harm. If a phrase matches, the app shows crisis-support resources before anything else happens. The check runs locally, its result is never stored, logged or sent anywhere, and no human or AI is alerted. This is a safety net, not a clinical assessment. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services right away (Korea: 112/119, or the suicide prevention line 109; US: call or text 988; UK and Ireland: Samaritans on 116 123).

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and, in our cloud database, at rest. Database-level row security means that only your own authenticated session can read or write your account's data. API keys are kept on the server and never sent to your device. We limit internal access to what is necessary. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we will notify you and the relevant authorities of any breach affecting your information as the law requires. Under PIPA, that means notifying you within 72 hours of discovery.

Data stored as a guest lives in your browser's storage. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to see it, so protect your device with a passcode.
